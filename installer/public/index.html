ONE-TIME SETUP

Make it yours.

A USB data cable, home Wi-Fi, and a few minutes.

Open this page in desktop Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge to use USB setup. Safari and Firefox do not support this connection.
01

Connect your display

Plug in your E1001 with a USB data cable. Close other serial monitors before connecting.

No display selected.

02

Connect to home

Use the network’s name, not its xx:xx:xx address.

03

Choose your arrivals

Two rows, with the next two arrivals on each. Choose the same line twice for both directions, or mix lines.

Get a free 511 key. Paste the full value after “Here is your token”. Both rows share one request every 61 seconds. Use a dedicated key for this display.

04

Add your calendars

Combine two Google calendars into one agenda. No Google sign-in on the display.

Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format. For a calendar someone shared with you, ask its owner for this link. An ID or public URL also works if the calendar is already public.

05

Set your rhythm

Pacific time, including daylight saving. Choosing the same start and end time means all day. Calendars check every 15 minutes during active hours. The image stays visible while the display sleeps.

You’re set.

The display is fetching your arrivals and calendars. Keep it powered for its first refresh. Then you can close this page—it runs on its own Wi-Fi.

Private calendar links grant read access. They and the Wi-Fi password are stored in the display’s flash. You can revoke a calendar link in Google’s settings.
